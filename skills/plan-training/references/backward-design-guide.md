# Backward Course Design & Bloom's Taxonomy Guide

This reference guide breaks down Stage 1 (Intended Learning Outcomes) and Stage 2 (Assessment Evidence) of the Wiggins & McTighe Backward Design framework for university STEM and technical courses.

---

## 1. Bloom's Revised Taxonomy Action Verbs

When writing Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs), use specific, observable, and measurable action verbs corresponding to the cognitive level required:

| Cognitive Level | Definition | Measurable Action Verbs | Non-Measurable Verbs to AVOID |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Remember** | Recall facts and basic concepts | Define, duplicate, list, memorize, name, repeat, state, identify | *Understand, know, be familiar with* |
| **2. Understand** | Explain ideas, mechanisms, or concepts | Classify, describe, discuss, explain, identify, locate, recognize, report, select, translate | *Learn, appreciate, grasp, see* |
| **3. Apply** | Use information in new situations | Execute, implement, solve, use, demonstrate, interpret, operate, schedule, sketch, compute | *Think about, explore, look at* |
| **4. Analyze** | Draw connections among ideas | Differentiate, organize, relate, compare, contrast, distinguish, examine, experiment, question, test | *Become aware of, get exposure to* |
| **5. Evaluate** | Justify a stand or decision | Appraise, argue, defend, judge, select, support, critique, evaluate, validate, verify | *Appreciate the beauty of* |
| **6. Create** | Produce new or original work | Design, assemble, construct, conjecture, develop, formulate, author, investigate, simulate | *Cover, master* |

---

## 2. The 3-Part ILO Formulation Formula

An effective ILO answers three questions:
1. **Condition**: *"By the end of this [course / unit / lecture]..."*
2. **Action**: *"...learners will be able to [Measurable Bloom's Verb]..."*
3. **Context / Criterion**: *"...[specific concept, tool, dataset, or problem regime] by [criterion of success]."*

### Examples:
- **Course-Level (Higher Bloom)**:
  - *"By the end of this course, learners will be able to **design and implement** a zero-dimensional planetary energy balance climate model in Python to **evaluate** the transient climate sensitivity resulting from greenhouse gas forcing scenarios."*
- **Unit-Level (Mid Bloom)**:
  - *"By the end of this unit, learners will be able to **differentiate** between convective and stratiform precipitation mechanisms using Doppler radar reflectivity cross-sections."*
- **Lesson-Level (Foundation Bloom)**:
  - *"By the end of today's lecture, learners will be able to **calculate** the blackbody emission temperature of a planetary body given its solar constant and surface albedo."*

## 3. Constructive Alignment Matrix

Ensure that for every stated ILO, there is a corresponding formative check, a summative assessment task, and an in-class active learning experience:

| Unit / Topic | Intended Learning Outcome (ILO) | Formative Check (Low-Stakes) | Summative Assessment (High-Stakes) | In-Class Learning Experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Planetary Energy Balance** | *Derive and solve* the 1D radiative equilibrium temperature equation. | Concept Test clicker poll on albedo sensitivity. | Problem Set 1: Radiative transfer derivations. | Interactive Python simulation notebook in pairs. |
| **Atmospheric Boundary Layer** | *Analyze* vertical potential temperature soundings to *diagnose* atmospheric stability. | Minute paper sketching stable vs. unstable profiles. | Midterm Exam: Sounding interpretation problem. | Think-Pair-Share on radiosonde data from Hong Kong. |
| **Extreme Weather Prediction** | *Evaluate* ensemble forecast dispersion to *quantify* predictive uncertainty. | Jigsaw breakout discussing spread-skill relationships. | Final Project: Build probabilistic forecast pipeline in Darts. | Group case study analyzing a historical typhoon event. |
