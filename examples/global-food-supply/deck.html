Key takeaways

  • Beat population: Output grew 257% vs. 164% population (1961–2023).
  • Land spared: Cereal area rose 14%; yield rose 212% to 4,228 kg/ha.
  • Method: Yield share is ln(yield ratio) / ln(production ratio), 1961–2023. Cereals proxy staple calories.

Key takeaways

  • Global drop: World rate fell 4.5 pp, from 12.7% in 2000 to 8.2% in 2024.
  • Africa outlier: Sub-Saharan Africa is 22.3%, nearly triple the global rate.
  • Capital implication: Shift funding from acreage to African yield and storage.
